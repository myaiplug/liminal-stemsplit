Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026 · MyAiPlug / Liminal StemSplit

Liminal StemSplit is designed for local-first audio processing. The desktop application processes audio on your computer. Your source audio files are not uploaded to our servers for separation.

Information we collect

  • Website: If you submit your email on our site, we store it to send product updates and onboarding information via Resend.
  • Purchases: Stripe processes payments. We receive your purchase email to issue license credentials.
  • Desktop app: Free-tier usage counts may be stored locally on your device. Pro activation validates your license against our license server using the email and access password you provide.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your audio to the cloud for stem separation in the desktop app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not bundle third-party adware in our installer.
