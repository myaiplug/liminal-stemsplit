Got Questions?

How does stem separation work? +
Liminal uses state-of-the-art AI models (Demucs, MDX, Roformer) trained on millions of songs. Each model analyzes the frequency and time patterns to isolate individual instruments. The desktop app runs these models locally on your own GPU/CPU.
Is my audio sent to the cloud? +
No. The desktop app processes everything on your computer. Your audio never touches the internet for separation. The website demo only streams pre-separated preview audio in your browser — it does not upload your files.
What's the quality compared to lalal.ai / Splitter.ai? +
Independent benchmarks show Liminal matches or exceeds cloud services on most tracks. With Demucs + MDX ensemble mode, you get studio-grade separation. And since it's local, there's no file size limit, no upload queue, and no monthly cap.
What are the system requirements? +
Windows 10+ or macOS 12+. 8GB RAM recommended (16GB for large batches). NVIDIA GPU with 4GB+ VRAM for fastest processing. CPU-only mode works but is slower. The app auto-detects your hardware and chooses the best settings.
