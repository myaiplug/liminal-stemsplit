Runs in your browser · WebGPU

Split stems right here.

State-of-the-art RoFormer models running on your own GPU, inside this tab. No upload, no server, no account. The model downloads once and stays in your browser.

How it works

  • Your audio is decoded and separated locally with onnxruntime-web in a background Web Worker. It never leaves your device.
  • Models are fetched directly from Hugging Face the first time you use them (SHA-256 checked), saved in your browser (resumable if interrupted) and reusable offline — delete them any time with “Clear downloaded models”.
  • Best on desktop Chrome or Edge with a dedicated GPU. Without WebGPU it falls back to the CPU, which works but is much slower — use the Fast preview model there.
