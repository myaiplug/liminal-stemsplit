Liminal StemSplit Full-length split Hear a finished demo

Split 20 seconds in this browser.

Drop a song. A 20 second stretch is chosen at random from the parts of the file where sub, drums, and vocal-range energy sit together. The RoFormer then splits only that stretch: vocals, drums, bass, and other. Nothing is uploaded. After it finishes, cut a lead vocal, backup / adlibs, an 808, and a deeper drum kit (kick, snare/clap, hi-hats, crash, perk) from those stems. The first run downloads the fast model (about 97 MB) into this browser.
